About Us
"We didn't start Keiz House because the market needed another agency.
We started it because we knew how to do it better."

Keiz House was built at the intersection of technology and storytelling two disciplines that, when wielded together, can turn an unknown brand into an industry name.

We came up through the agency world: pitching boardrooms, managing campaigns across continents, engineering systems that scaled. We've seen what works, what doesn't, and more importantly what's been left on the table.

Today, we bring that experience directly to the brands we believe in. No layers. No hand-offs to juniors. Just two people who've spent years building for others, now building for you.
